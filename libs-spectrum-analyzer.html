Reproducible by design

Simple processing with visible assumptions.

  1. 01

    Parse

    Numeric wavelength and intensity columns are read directly in the browser; files are not uploaded to a server.

  2. 02

    Correct

    An optional linear baseline is estimated from median intensities at the two spectrum edges and subtracted.

  3. 03

    Normalize

    Each sample can be divided by its maximum or integrated area to support visual comparison.

  4. 04

    Review

    Local maxima above the selected threshold are ranked for inspection and export.
