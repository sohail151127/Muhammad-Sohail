Required caution

Temperature and electron density are model-dependent estimates. Meeting the McWhirter criterion supports one necessary LTE condition but does not by itself prove local thermodynamic equilibrium, optical thinness, spatial homogeneity, correct line identification or absence of self-absorption.

Reporting discipline

A report should make uncertainty visible.

  1. 01
    Do not combine mismatched measurements

    Temperature and density should represent compatible plasma regions, delays and detector gates.

  2. 02
    Keep estimates distinct from observations

    A fitted line width is measured; electron density inferred from it is a model-dependent estimate.

  3. 03
    Record what remains unresolved

    An unchecked validity item is reported as not documented—not silently assumed to be satisfied.

  4. 04
    Preserve traceability

    Keep the original spectra, fit settings, atomic references and processing steps with the research record.
