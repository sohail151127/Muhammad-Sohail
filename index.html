MPhil research · University of Agriculture Faisalabad

Comparative Nutritional Characterization of Healthy and Virus-Infected Plant Samples

The study examined changes in elemental profiles using LIBS, AAS, calibration-free LIBS and principal component analysis. It combined experimental laser spectroscopy with quantitative validation and multivariate interpretation.
