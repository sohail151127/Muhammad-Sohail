Understand the decision

What the checker actually says.

It compares collision-supporting electron density with a threshold derived from temperature and the hardest relevant excitation step.

  1. 01

    Temperature changes the threshold slowly

    The requirement grows with √T. A fourfold increase in temperature doubles this part of the threshold.

  2. 02

    Energy gap changes it strongly

    The requirement grows with (ΔE)³. Doubling the energy gap increases the minimum density eightfold.

  3. 03

    Density is compared directly

    If measured Ne is greater than Ne,min, the McWhirter condition is met for the entered quantities.

  4. 04

    Uncertainty tests a cautious case

    The conservative margin compares the lower density bound with the upper required-density bound. It is still not a complete scientific uncertainty model.

Beyond one inequality

What else LTE needs.

LIBS plasmas change rapidly in time and space. A responsible analysis treats McWhirter as one item in a larger validity argument.

  • Spatial homogeneityTemperature and density should not vary so strongly across the observed volume that one local value becomes misleading.
  • Temporal suitabilityThe detector delay and gate should sample a period when equilibrium processes are fast relative to plasma evolution.
  • Optically thin linesSelf-absorption alters intensities and can invalidate Boltzmann or Saha–Boltzmann interpretations.
  • Consistent temperaturesExcitation, ionization and kinetic descriptions should be compatible with the equilibrium model being used.
  • Reliable diagnosticsTemperature, density and energy levels must come from suitable lines, atomic data and transparent fitting procedures.
  • Relevant energy gapThe selected ΔE must correspond to the level system for which equilibrium is being evaluated.

Beginner glossary

The vocabulary behind LTE.

LTE
Local thermodynamic equilibrium: an approximation in which particle populations within a small plasma region can be described by thermodynamic relations.
McWhirter criterion
A minimum-electron-density test based on whether collisions can compete sufficiently with radiative processes for selected energy levels.
Necessary condition
A requirement that must be met, but which cannot establish the full conclusion by itself.
Sufficient condition
A condition that, when valid in its stated setting, is enough to support a conclusion. McWhirter is generally not sufficient for a transient LIBS plasma.
Energy gap, ΔE
The energy separation between two relevant atomic levels, entered in eV or spectroscopic wavenumber (cm⁻¹).
Consecutive levels
Neighbouring levels in the energy-level sequence being considered—not necessarily the upper and lower levels of any arbitrary observed line.
Nominal margin
The measured density divided by the minimum required density using the central input values.
Conservative margin
The lower measured-density bound divided by the upper required-density bound using the entered one-standard-deviation uncertainties.
Scientific basis and unit conversion

The LIBS reviews give the McWhirter equation and emphasize its limitations. NIST ASD provides evaluated energy levels, while NIST CODATA provides the energy–wavenumber relationship used for cm⁻¹ conversion.
